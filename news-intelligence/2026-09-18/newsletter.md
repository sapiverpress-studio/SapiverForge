## 1. OpenAI targets legal sector with 'Astra for Law'

**Confirmed:** OpenAI has launched 'Astra for Law,' an enterprise platform built on the GPT-6 Astra model that integrates legal search indices from the Free Law Project and ecosystem plugins for tools like Relativity and Clio.

**Why it matters:** It marks a significant push by OpenAI into specialised professional services, though the platform requires firms to manage their own compliance and human oversight.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The partnership with CourtListener suggests a focus on grounding AI outputs in verifiable legal data, a necessary step for enterprise adoption in a field where hallucinations are a liability.

## 2. OpenAI internal repositories compromised in exploit chain

**Confirmed:** Security researchers at Hacktron AI successfully compromised OpenAI employee accounts by chaining a heap overflow vulnerability in libheif with an SSO misconfiguration, gaining access to internal GitHub repositories.

**Why it matters:** The incident highlights that even major AI labs are susceptible to standard software vulnerabilities, with the researchers demonstrating access by submitting a pull request to an internal monorepo.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** While OpenAI patched the flaws within 72 hours, the ability to reach internal code repositories through a forum-based image processing vulnerability is a stark reminder of the interconnected nature of modern development environments.

**Source:** [hacktron.ai](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/hacking-openai) · discovered via Hacker News · confidence 98%

## 3. Anthropic moves into physical biology with new wet lab

**Confirmed:** Anthropic has established a physical 'wet lab' in the San Francisco Bay Area to conduct biological experiments, following its acquisition of Coefficient Bio.

**Why it matters:** This signals a shift for frontier AI labs from purely digital model training to integrating physical experimental feedback loops into their research processes.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** By moving into physical lab space, Anthropic is attempting to bridge the gap between theoretical model predictions and real-world biological validation, a move that suggests they are serious about the practical application of their models in life sciences.
