Privacy, sound and grown-up controls

Track names, patterns, tempo, swing and saved loops stay in local storage in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded and no account, microphone, camera or external service is used. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see the saved track names.

The sounds are generated by the browser only after a person presses Play. A grown-up should choose a comfortable device volume, especially when headphones are used. Downloaded pattern files contain settings rather than recorded audio and are then managed through the device’s normal files and sharing controls.
