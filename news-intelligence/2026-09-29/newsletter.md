**Confirmed:** AMD has agreed to acquire spatial intelligence startup World Labs in an all-stock deal, with co-founder Dr. Fei-Fei Li joining as Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist.

**Why it matters:** Integrating World Labs' 3D environment generation research into AMD’s hardware roadmap signals a push to move beyond standard LLMs into spatial and world-model AI capabilities.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** Bringing in a pioneer like Dr. Fei-Fei Li is a clear signal that AMD wants to be more than just a chip supplier; they are positioning themselves to own the software-hardware stack for the next generation of AI environments.

## 3. Anthropic Launches Claude Sonnet 5.5

**Confirmed:** Anthropic has released Claude Sonnet 5.5, which the company claims is 30% faster and more cost-efficient than its predecessor, with improved performance on agentic coding benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** For developers, the update introduces breaking API changes, including new requirements for tool calls and thinking block streams, which will require immediate code adjustments.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** Anthropic is clearly prioritising agentic workflows, but the breaking changes are a reminder that 'faster and cheaper' often comes with the headache of refactoring your existing integrations.

## 4. New Legislation Targets Frontier AI Liability

**Confirmed:** Rep. Ro Khanna has introduced the 'Human Control Over AI Act,' which proposes strict liability for developers, a ban on recursive self-improving models, and mandatory on-site federal auditors.

**Why it matters:** While the bill is unlikely to pass before the midterms, it establishes a formal legislative benchmark for how regulators might seek to enforce safety and accountability in the future.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The proposal is a shot across the bow for labs that have previously operated with significant autonomy. It signals that the 'move fast' era of AI development is facing a serious push for federal oversight.
