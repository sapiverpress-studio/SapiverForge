# Sources for Intel D270: 160GB on Intel's card, up to 480GB through partners

- Intel Newsroom, 12 October 2026: https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/artificial-intelligence/intel-accelerates-open-infrastructure-agentic-ai-era.html
- Intel confirms its own branded card has 160GB, while ODM/partner cards may offer up to 480GB (footnote 1).
- ServeTheHome technical breakdown, 12 October: https://www.servethehome.com/intels-crescent-island-ai-server-gpu-gets-a-name-and-specs-meet-the-intel-gpu-d-series/
- Intel claims up to 1.5TB/s and 1.3PFLOPS FP8 at 350W. These are vendor specifications, not independently proven end-to-end costs or throughput.
- No published mass-shipping timetable or comparative production benchmarks have been verified for this edition.
