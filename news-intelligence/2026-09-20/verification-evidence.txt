#### **Candidate [15]: Meta, Roblox, and Quora Challenge Ofcom’s Online Safety Act Categorisation**
* **Publisher:** Financial Times (Daniel Thomas)
* **What is confirmed:** Meta has filed a new legal appeal in the UK Upper Tribunal challenging Ofcom’s decision to designate WhatsApp and Instagram as "Category 1" services under the UK Online Safety Act (OSA). Meta argues that Category 1 requirements—such as measures against fraudulent advertising and reporting on illegal content—are unsuitable for private and small-group messaging platforms. Roblox and Quora are pursuing separate tribunal appeals against their respective categorisations. This adds to ongoing High Court judicial review proceedings brought by Meta against Ofcom regarding fee and fine structures.
* **Important caveats:** UK politicians and policy advocates express concern that tech companies are using litigation to delay implementation of the OSA. Ofcom has not yet publicly published a full formal response to the tribunal filing.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **Yes.** Highly relevant to UK tech regulation, digital business, and platform policy.

* **Publisher:** Reuters / South China Morning Post
* **What is confirmed:** ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China's top DRAM manufacturer, announced at the 2026 World Manufacturing Convention in Hefei that its fifth-generation (G5) technology platform has entered mass production. * **Important caveats:** Performance and density claims relative to global market leaders (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) represent company assertions. Due to US export controls on EUV equipment, CXMT relies on multi-exposure DUV techniques.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **Yes.** Significant breakthrough for hardware, global semiconductor competition, and tech supply chains.
