How to play

1Tap a gap between two neighbouring dots to draw one line.
2Complete the fourth side of a box to claim it and play again.
3When the board is full, the player with most boxes wins.

Strategy without pressure

Look for nearly completed boxes, but remember that every line might leave an opportunity for the other player. Small boards are quick; larger boards allow longer chains and more planning.

Scores are informal family game results. Keep turns friendly and use Undo when somebody taps the wrong line.

Private by design

Player names, colours, match scores and up to eight recent rounds stay in local browser storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, camera, microphone or external services.

Clearing browser data removes the saved settings and scores.
