research · anthropic.com

Claude solves 25-year-old cryptography mystery

What happened: Anthropic researchers used an internal model to trace the seeds of NIST's standard ECDSA curves to a 1990s Unix timestamp and a standard C library pseudorandom number generator. The finding confirms the seeds were not generated via a secret passphrase.

Why it matters: It resolves a long-standing cryptographic uncertainty, making intentional backdoors in these standards significantly less plausible.

Our read: A rare moment where AI capability actually helps us verify the security foundations we have been relying on for decades.

ai · mercor.com

The AI benchmark gap

What happened: Mercor's analysis of 29 agentic AI benchmarks against US labor data suggests that approximately 90% of American working hours are spent on tasks not currently represented in these benchmarks.

Why it matters: It highlights a significant disconnect between what AI is currently tested for and the actual, messy reality of most human work.

Our read: Benchmarks are useful for measuring progress in a lab, but they are currently a poor proxy for the actual economic impact of AI in the wild.

business · Kyohei Suga/Nikkei Asia

Kioxia's market value slide

What happened: Japanese NAND memory maker Kioxia has seen its market capitalization drop to one-third of its June 2026 peak, pressured by rising supply from Chinese competitors and broader data center investment concerns.

Why it matters: It signals a potential cooling in the semiconductor supply chain, even as AI demand remains a factor for near-term profitability.

Our read: The market is re-rating memory chipmakers as the initial AI-driven supply frenzy meets the reality of increased global competition.

business · Reuters

NYC Comptroller calls for Anthropic IPO pause

What happened: NYC Comptroller Mark Levine has formally requested that Anthropic pause its planned US IPO, citing concerns over frontier model safety and governance speed.

Why it matters: As an institutional investor, the Comptroller's office is applying public pressure on the governance of high-stakes AI labs.

Our read: This is a clear signal that the financial and regulatory scrutiny of frontier AI labs is intensifying as they move toward public markets.
