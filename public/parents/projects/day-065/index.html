Privacy and important limits

Everything you add is saved only in local storage in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded and the app cannot see your home, photographs, files or location. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to view the saved directory, and clearing browser data may remove it.

Avoid recording alarm codes, safe combinations or other security secrets. The finder only repeats the places you enter; it cannot confirm that an item is still there. Use another reliable system for medication, emergency equipment or anything safety-critical.
