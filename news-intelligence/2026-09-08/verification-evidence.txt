#### Candidate [5]: China Targets 9,800 Eflops AI Compute Capacity by 2030
* **Confirmed:** China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) released a 2026–2030 five-year plan targeting 9,800 eflops of intelligent compute capacity by 2030. The plan calls for 3.8 trillion yuan (~$532B) in cumulative IT infrastructure investment and sets targets for 100,000-card AI accelerator clusters. MIIT reported that China reached 2,185 eflops by the end of June 2026 (up 177% YoY) across 52 major computing facilities.
* **Caveats:** Figures are government self-reported statistics and targets; compute capacity metrics ("eflops") can vary depending on precision levels (e.g., FP16 vs INT8).
* **Publication Suitability:** **Yes.**

#### Candidate [6]: AI-Assisted Zero-Click "WeWorm" Flaw Patched by Tencent
* **Confirmed:** Cybersecurity research firm Calif demonstrated an AI-developed zero-click worm ("WeWorm") targeting WeChat across iOS and Android. The exploit took advantage of a memory-corruption vulnerability in WeChat's VoIP stack. Tencent confirmed the vulnerability and deployed fixes across Android, iOS, and server infrastructure.
* **Caveats:** Developed as a proof-of-concept in a controlled research setting; there is no indication that the worm was deployed maliciously in the wild.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Yes.**

#### Candidate [7]: ASML, TSMC, Samsung, and Intel Commit to High-NA EUV & 12-Inch Photomasks
* **Confirmed:** ASML, TSMC, Samsung, and Intel announced a joint initiative to shift semiconductor lithography from 6-inch to 12-inch photomasks for High-NA EUV tools. Samsung announced plans to deploy High-NA EUV for DRAM memory production by 2028; TSMC committed to High-NA adoption for advanced nodes in 2030. The group targets a 12-inch mask pilot line by 2031 and production readiness by 2033. ASML projects that 12-inch masks will prevent stitching seam errors on large AI chips and boost machine throughput by up to 40%.
* **Caveats:** Full production deployment of 12-inch photomasks is a long-term goal (2033) requiring extensive re-tooling across the supply chain. High-NA EUV tools carry a unit price of ~$400 million.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Yes.**
