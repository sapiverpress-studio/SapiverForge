Family Choice Board

Compare the choices your family is considering against the things that genuinely matter to you—then see how the result was calculated.

Our choices

Name this choice

For example: Saturday outing, new family tent, or after-school club.

Add options

Add at least two real possibilities.

Add priorities

Importance multiplies each rating, so the priorities that matter most count more.

Rate each choice

Give each option 1–5 for every priority. One means a poor fit; five means an excellent fit.

Your comparison

The score is each rating multiplied by that priority's importance. Nothing is hidden.

A useful aid, not the decision-maker. Talk through practical details, safety, affordability and how everyone feels before choosing.

Private by design

This app has no account and sends nothing anywhere. Your board, ratings and results stay in local storage in this browser. Clearing browser data removes them.
