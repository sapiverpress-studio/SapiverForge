### **Candidate [6] — Mass cyberattack on European supermarket refrigeration systems**
* **Publisher:** Ars Technica / BleepingComputer
* **Scope:** IN SCOPE (Cybersecurity / Industrial IoT)
* **What is confirmed:** A mass cyberattack targeted internet-connected refrigeration controllers (primarily legacy Danfoss AK-SM 800 models) at roughly 700 supermarkets in France (including Intermarché, Netto, and Carrefour stores). Attackers forced the systems into defrost mode, driving temperatures up to 50°C, causing tens of millions of euros in spoiled inventory and triggering localized electrical fires and smoke alarms.
* **Important caveats:** The security flaws exploited (including CVE-2025-41450 and CVE-2025-41451) were previously documented by Claroty Team82 and CISA in mid-2025; the vulnerability stemmed from outdated, end-of-life devices left exposed to the open internet without patches. Official attribution of the attackers remains under active investigation by French cyber agency ANSSI.
* **Publication assessment:** **Suitable for publication today.**
