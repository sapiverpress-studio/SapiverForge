How it works

The app plays one sound for every active beat. Silent spaces are rests. After the count-in, tap the large pad on the beats you heard. You are scored on which beat positions you copied, not on millisecond-perfect timing.

Private by design

This game runs entirely in this browser. Difficulty, tempo, score, streak and best score stay only in local browser storage on this device. No microphone is used, no audio is recorded and nothing is uploaded.

For grown-ups

Choose a comfortable volume before starting and keep it playful. Browser audio may require the first button tap before sound can play.

Build your own with AI

Build me a mobile-friendly copy-the-rhythm game as one standalone HTML file. Let a parent choose 4, 6 or 8 beats, a tempo and difficulty. Generate patterns containing sounds and rests, play them with the Web Audio API, give a clear count-in, let the child tap the rhythm back on a large button, compare taps by beat position, give immediate accuracy feedback, track score and streak, and save settings locally.
