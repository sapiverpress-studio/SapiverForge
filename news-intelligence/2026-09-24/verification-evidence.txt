#### **[1] Anthropic: Claude discovers novel enzyme system in bacteriophages**
* **Confirmed:** Anthropic announced that a team of autonomous Claude AI agents analyzed a database of 1.9 billion protein clusters and identified a previously undescribed enzyme system in bacteriophage DNA, which they named "array-associated reverse transcriptases" (ART). The system features a reverse transcriptase gene adjacent to a non-coding repeating DNA array similar in structure to CRISPR.
* **Caveats:** Findings are documented in a preprint that has not yet undergone peer review. Anthropic's team has not yet experimentally demonstrated wet-lab activity or the precise biological mechanism of the enzyme.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Suitable.** Strong landmark story for AI-driven biological discovery.

* **Caveats:** Priced at $1,299, the device is scheduled for launch in Spring 2027 rather than immediate commercial availability.
