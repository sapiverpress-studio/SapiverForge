#### Candidates `[1]` & `[3]`: TypeSafe AI Launches "System One" Model Jev
* **Confirmed:** TypeSafe AI announced its new model architecture series "System One Models" and its debut model, Jev. Unlike standard generative LLMs, Jev bypasses text generation to produce direct, typed probabilistic decisions and confidence scores for software integration. The company has raised $40M in funding.
* **Caveats:** Jev is closed-weight and available only via an early-access waitlist API. The claim of "0% structural hallucination" refers strictly to schema adherence (it cannot return malformed JSON or invalid types), but its output values can still be factually incorrect.
* **Suitability for Publication:** **Suitable for publication today.** High reader interest for backend engineering and AI architectural shifts.
