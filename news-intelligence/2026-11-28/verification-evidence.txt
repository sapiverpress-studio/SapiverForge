#### **Candidate [5]: Google Research details DeepCrossAttention (DCA) for Transformer architectures**
* **What is confirmed:** Google Research published a technical overview detailing DeepCrossAttention (DCA), an architectural revision to Transformer residual skip connections presented at ICML 2025. DCA replaces traditional point-wise residual addition with dynamic, per-token routing and depth-wise cross-attention across previous layers. Google reports DCA achieves equivalent model quality up to 3x faster in training time (or up to 5x fewer steps) while adding under 0.2% extra parameters. DCA can also be retrofitted onto existing pre-trained checkpoints.
* **Important caveats:** Performance gains and layer reduction figures (e.g., 29% depth reduction on LM1B) are drawn from Google's internal benchmarking on LM1B and C4 datasets, rather than independent evaluations on frontier-scale models. DCA increases memory-bandwidth overhead because intermediate representations from preceding layers must be preserved.
* **Publication status:** **Suitable for publication today.**

#### **Candidate [6]: Power operators adopt dynamic line rating (DLR) software for AI grid demand**
* **What is confirmed:** Financial Times reporting by Martha Muir confirms power grid operators in North America and Europe are deploying Dynamic Line Rating (DLR) systems—combining field sensors and weather-aware software—to dynamically adjust transmission safety limits. By calculating real-time conductor cooling from wind and ambient temperatures, operators are boosting existing transmission line capacity by 8% to 30% to accommodate surging electricity demand from AI data centers.
* **Important caveats:** DLR optimizes existing transmission efficiency rather than generating new power supply. Capacity gains fluctuate based on localized weather conditions and require ongoing sensor maintenance.
* **Publication status:** **Suitable for publication today.**
