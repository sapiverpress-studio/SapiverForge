Today’s edition looks at Anthropic’s IPO economics, AMD’s World Labs acquisition, Claude Sonnet 5.5 migration changes and a proposed federal AI-control bill.

Anthropic IPO filing shows rapid growth alongside heavy losses and compute commitments

What happened: Reuters reports that Anthropic’s draft IPO prospectus shows 2025 revenue of about $4.6 billion, an operating loss of about $8.06 billion and a net loss of about $42 billion, including roughly $34 billion of accounting charges. The filing also discloses about $518 billion of future cloud and infrastructure spending commitments, while roughly a quarter of revenue came from two customers.

Why it matters: Operating losses, non-cash accounting charges, infrastructure commitments and customer concentration are separate risks and should not be collapsed into one headline figure.

AMD agrees to acquire World Labs for $8.2 billion

AMD has agreed to acquire World Labs in an all-stock deal valued at about $8.2 billion. Dr. Fei-Fei Li is set to join AMD as Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist.

Open World Labs source

Anthropic releases Claude Sonnet 5.5 with migration changes for some integrations

Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 generates tokens more than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and can cost up to 30% less per task in its testing. Breaking migration changes affect specific API behaviours rather than every integration.

Open Anthropic source

Khanna plans Human Control Over AI Act

CNBC reports that Rep. Ro Khanna plans to introduce the Human Control Over AI Act. It is a proposal, not enacted law, and floor votes are not expected before the midterm elections.

Open CNBC source

Practical takeaway

If you use Anthropic’s API, check the Sonnet 5.5 migration notes against the specific tool-call and thinking-block behaviours your integration uses before switching models. When assessing frontier-AI providers, separate operating performance from non-cash accounting charges and consider infrastructure commitments and customer concentration alongside revenue growth.

What to watch next

Watch for Anthropic’s IPO filing to move through SEC review, further detail on its infrastructure commitments and customer concentration, real-world Sonnet 5.5 migration reports, and for Khanna to formally file the Human Control Over AI Act and publish its final legislative text.
