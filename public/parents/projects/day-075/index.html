Private by design

This app does not use a camera, photos, an account, analytics or an external service. Your current choices and up to eight saved avatars stay in this browser’s local storage. A downloaded PNG is handled by your device like any other file.

A grown-up should check names and pictures before downloading or sharing. Browser storage can be cleared and is not a permanent backup.
