Private by design

Photos and project settings stay in this browser’s IndexedDB storage. Nothing is uploaded and no account, advertising, analytics or external service is used. Selecting “use camera” is handled by the device’s own photo picker.

A grown-up checks the scene and pictures before saving or sharing. Browser storage is not a permanent backup; exported WebM files are managed through the device’s normal files and sharing controls.
