The magnetic pole rule

Different poles attractNorth facing South pulls the magnets together.
Matching poles repelNorth facing North, or South facing South, pushes them apart.
Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole. Cutting a magnet does not leave a single loose pole—it makes smaller magnets, each with both poles. This screen game models pole interactions and does not measure real magnetic strength.
