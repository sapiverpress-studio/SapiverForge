What makes the bulb light?

1 · Energy sourceA battery supplies energy to the circuit.
2 · Complete loopWires must make an unbroken path from one battery terminal to the other.
3 · Closed switchAn open switch breaks the path. A closed switch completes it.
Safety: this is a screen simulation using simple low-voltage circuit ideas. Children should never experiment with mains electricity, sockets or damaged electrical items.
