### **[0] Denmark Data Breach Exposes Personal Data of 8.8M People**
* **What is confirmed:** Denmark’s Ministry of Research, Education and Digitalization confirmed a major cybersecurity breach of the Central Person Register (CPR). Unauthorized actors accessed names, addresses, and 10-digit CPR identification numbers for approximately 8.8 million registered individuals during September 2026 (detected Oct 2). 
* **Important caveats:** The compromise did not result from a direct breach of core government servers. Instead, attackers illicitly abused legitimate database search permissions granted to a private Danish company. The total CPR database contains ~11 million records (including deceased individuals and emigrants) relative to Denmark's resident population of ~6 million. Citizens registered under name and address protection were not exposed.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **Yes.** A critical infrastructure security breach impacting national civil registration data.
