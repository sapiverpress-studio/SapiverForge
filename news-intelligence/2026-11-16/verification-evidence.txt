#### **[12] US Commerce Department Restricts Remote Cloud Access to Advanced AI Chips (Bloomberg / TechCrunch / BIS)**

* **Important Caveats:** This is an interim final rule with a 60-day public comment window and a 90-day grace period for existing data center operators to secure licenses.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Suitable.** Critical story on international technology regulation, semiconductor supply chains, and AI trade policy.
