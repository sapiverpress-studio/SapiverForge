### [1] Google DeepMind releases AlphaGenome Atlas
* **Candidate:** [1]
* **What is confirmed:** Google DeepMind launched AlphaGenome Atlas, a free, 1-petabyte database providing precomputed molecular effect predictions for all ~9 billion possible single-nucleotide DNA variants in the human genome. It introduces the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score to help researchers prioritize variants across coding and non-coding DNA.
* **Important caveats:** Predictions are generated *in silico* by DeepMind's underlying AlphaGenome model and require downstream laboratory/clinical validation, though early collaborations with the Broad Institute and UK Biobank demonstrated positive diagnostic utility.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **YES.** Significant release in AI applied to biological research and medicine.
