Build your own with AI

Build me a mobile-friendly shadow-learning game in one standalone HTML file. Let a parent choose a child’s name, a familiar object, direction or length questions, and a game length. Show the Sun on the left, high overhead or on the right; ask which way the shadow points or whether it is long or short; reveal an accurate shadow after each answer; explain that shadows point away from the light and lower light creates longer shadows; track scores and streaks; save results locally; and do not use location, camera, weather or external services.
