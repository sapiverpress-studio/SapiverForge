Build your own with AI
Build me a mobile-friendly personalised sentence-building game as one standalone HTML file. Give a parent setup area for adding and deleting custom subjects, actions, things and places. In play mode, show large colour-coded word tiles, let a child choose one from each category, check that all four parts are present, assemble a punctuated sentence, read it aloud using browser speech synthesis, track score, streak and best streak, keep a recent correct-sentence history and save everything only in local browser storage.
