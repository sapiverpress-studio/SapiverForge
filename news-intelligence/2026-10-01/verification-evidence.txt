#### **[15] California Signs "No Robo Bosses Act" (SB 947) Banning AI-Only Firings**
* **Confirmed:** California Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 947 into law, making California the first US state to prohibit employers from relying solely on automated decision systems (AI) to fire or discipline workers. Where AI serves as the primary basis for disciplinary action, human reviewers must independently corroborate the finding using secondary records.
* **Caveats:** The law takes effect on July 1, 2027. Pre-notification requirements were removed following a veto of a broader 2025 bill, and gig-worker provisions were scaled back.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Suitable for publication today.**
