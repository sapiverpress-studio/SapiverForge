How it works

The app slices one picture into a numbered grid in memory. It starts solved, then makes many legal empty-square moves, so every shuffled puzzle remains solvable. Tap a neighbouring piece to slide it into the gap.

Privacy

This app runs entirely in your browser. If you choose a device picture, the browser reads it only into the current tab so it can draw the puzzle; the app does not upload it or save the image in local storage. Player name, selected size, built-in scene choice and best runs stay in local browser storage. There is no account, advertising, camera request, microphone, location, analytics or external service. Closing the tab removes the chosen photo from the app.
