#### Candidate [0]: Mistral releases Mistral Large 4 ("le Chonk") 1T-parameter model
* **What is confirmed:** On October 6, 2026, Mistral AI released a public preview of **Mistral Large 4 (ML4)**, code-named **"Le Chonk"**. The 1-trillion parameter multimodal Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model uses 49 billion active parameters per token. It was trained over two months across 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in European data centers. Preview access is live today via Mistral's API, with a focus on agentic workloads, coding, and cybersecurity.
* **Important caveats:** Open model weights are not downloadable today; they are scheduled for public release on October 27, 2026, following a 3-week preview and testing period with cybersecurity partners and government bodies. The weights will be distributed under a custom Mistral license rather than pure open-source terms.
* **Publication readiness:** **Suitable for publication today.** This is a major flagship AI model release from Europe's leading frontier lab.

#### Candidate [5]: Reflection AI announces Beam (501B open-weight model)
* **What is confirmed:** Reflection AI announced **Beam** on October 5, 2026, its first open-weight sparse MoE model. Beam features 501B total parameters with 23B active parameters per token, optimized for coding, reasoning, and agentic workflows. It was pretrained on 23.8 trillion tokens using 6,144 Nvidia GB300 GPUs.
* **Important caveats:** Weights are not yet available for local hosting or download; access is restricted to an API waitlist preview, with open weights promised under Apache 2.0 later in October 2026. Performance claims (e.g., matching GLM 5.2 at lower compute) rely on self-reported lab benchmarks.
* **Publication readiness:** **Suitable for publication today.**

#### Candidate [6]: Opus 5.5 agents identify room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates
* **What is confirmed:** AI benchmark/eval startup **Vals AI** published a report stating that an automated workflow of 90+ Claude Opus 5.5 agents ran quantum Density Functional Theory (DFT) simulations to identify two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor (RTMS) candidates: $\text{YBaMnFeO}_5$ and $\text{KV[Cr(CN)}_6\text{]}$.

Must be explicitly reported as an AI lab simulation showcase, not a validated physical discovery.
* **Publication readiness:** **Suitable for publication today**, with strict framing around computational/simulation status.
