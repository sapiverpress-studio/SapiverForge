Rotation settings

Recent rotations

Made for your home

Add only the toys you want to track. Use your own category names and notes for cupboards, boxes or shelves.

How suggestions work

The board suggests the stored toy with the oldest recorded move date. It does not judge developmental value or decide what your child should play with.

Privacy

Toy names, locations, notes, settings and rotation history are stored only in this browser using local storage. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account, advertising or analytics. Clearing browser data removes them.
