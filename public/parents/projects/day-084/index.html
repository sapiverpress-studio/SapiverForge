Helpful measuring notes

Measure the widest and deepest points of each item. Leave comfortable walking space and check doors, drawers, windows, radiators, sockets and assembly instructions before moving real furniture.

The planner is a visual guide, not a construction drawing. Always verify every measurement in the actual room.

Private by design

Your room name, dimensions and furniture layout stay in local browser storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, camera, microphone or external services.

Exported PNG files are handled by your browser and device. Clearing browser data removes the saved layout.
