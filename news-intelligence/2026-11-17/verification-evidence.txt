## Evidence Notes for Strongest In-Scope Candidates

### Candidate [8]: Reuters Investigation on Chinese AI Distillation of US Models
* **What is confirmed:** A Reuters review of over 300 Chinese academic papers, corporate filings, patents, and government documents revealed that AI model "distillation"—using outputs from frontier US models (such as OpenAI's o1, GPT-4, and GPT-4o) to train smaller, cheaper domestic models (e.g., Alibaba's Qwen)—has moved from academic experimentation into industrial-scale production in China. The practice is explicitly supported by Beijing's national AI+Manufacturing policy guidelines issued in January.
* **Important caveats:** Knowledge distillation is a standard machine learning technique globally and is not illegal under current U.S. export law, though U.S. officials argue industrial-scale distillation circumvents the intent of hardware export controls and violates API terms of service. Distillation transfers specific task capabilities to smaller student models but does not replicate full frontier model architecture or eliminate the need for base compute.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **YES.** High-impact exclusive investigation with direct consequences for global AI policy, trade regulations, and U.S.-China chip controls.

### Candidate [1]: Windows 11 Telemetry Tracking AI Coding Agents & Developer Tools
* **What is confirmed:** Reverse-engineering research published by developer Xusheng Li reveals that a Windows 11 system component (`MissionControlAggregator.dll`, under feature flag `CLICodingAgentUsage`) explicitly checks for the execution of AI coding agents (`claude.exe`, `copilot.exe`, `codex.exe`, `cursor.exe`, `antigravity.exe`, `agy.exe`) and standard developer tools (`python.exe`, `dotnet.exe`, `gcc.exe`, etc.). It logs a presence bitmap locally and queues it for Windows Diagnostics.
* **Important caveats:** The telemetry collects tool presence (binary flags indicating whether a tool ran), **not** prompt history, source code, or session transcripts. Transmitting this data to Microsoft requires "Optional Diagnostic Data" to be enabled, though researchers note Optional Diagnostics was turned on by default in clean U.S. ISO installs. Microsoft has not issued an official public statement.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **YES.** Highly relevant for developers, enterprise IT security leads, and data privacy officers.

### Candidate [11]: Nvidia Unveils Rubin HPC GPU at Supercomputing 2026
* **What is confirmed:** At the Supercomputing 2026 (SC26) conference, Nvidia announced the "Rubin HPC" GPU, a specialized processor designed for agentic scientific workflows and complex simulations. * **Suitable for publication today:** **YES.** Major hardware roadmap announcement from the leading AI semiconductor vendor.
