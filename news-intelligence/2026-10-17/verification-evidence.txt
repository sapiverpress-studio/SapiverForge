#### **[0] Claude reveals ECDSA curve origins**
* **What is confirmed:** On October 16, 2026, Anthropic published research demonstrating that an internal Claude research model solved a 25-year-old cryptography mystery by tracing the seeds of NIST's standard ECDSA curves (P-192, P-224, P-256, P-384, P-521). The seeds were generated using the standard C library's `lrand48` pseudorandom number generator initialized with 1997–1998 Unix timestamps, rather than a secret passphrase or complex hash. Cryptographer Filippo Valsorda independently verified the solution, confirming the $36,864 bounty was claimed and donated to the Internet Security Research Group (ISRG).
* **Important caveats:** Anthropic explicitly confirmed this is **not** a cryptographic break or vulnerability in ECDSA, and users do not need to take action or change security practices. While it makes intentional backdoors far less plausible, it cannot completely rule out subtle timestamp selection filtering.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **Yes.** Outstanding research milestone at the intersection of AI capability and cybersecurity history.
