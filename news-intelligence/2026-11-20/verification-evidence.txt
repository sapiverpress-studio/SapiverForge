* **What is confirmed:** Startup Architect Labs published a blog post introducing "Frostbite," a CPU microarchitecture based on interaction nets discovered, designed, and verified end-to-end by autonomous AI agent swarms. They report a 4.59x performance-per-watt advantage across 25 benchmark workloads compared to a 2-wide in-order superscalar baseline core.
* **Important caveats:** 
  * **Pre-silicon only:** The architecture has only reached 7nm RTL layout/FPGA simulation and has not been taped out or fabricated in physical silicon; performance and power metrics are model-estimated.
  * The baseline core used for comparison is a simple, low-power in-order core rather than a modern high-performance out-of-order CPU.
  * The findings are self-reported company claims without third-party hardware validation.
* **Suitable for publication today?** **Yes**, as a notable technical milestone in AI-assisted hardware design, provided pre-silicon and baseline limitations are clearly highlighted.
