Privacy and grown-up checks

Names, balances, goals and ledger entries are saved only in this browser using local storage. There is no account and nothing is sent elsewhere. Clearing browser data removes everything.

This is a family record, not a bank account. It cannot hold or move real money. A grown-up must check every entry, correct mistakes and keep any real cash or bank balance separately.
