# GPT-6.1 Sol: pricing, availability and the Critical cyber capability rating

A factual look at GPT-6.1 Sol's launch pricing, current availability, announced Ultrafast mode and OpenAI's Preparedness classification.

## What launched

OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol approaches GPT-6 Astra on several evaluations covering agentic coding, computer use and professional work, while charging one-fifth of Astra’s standard input and output token prices. Standard API pricing is two dollars per million input tokens and ten dollars per million output tokens.

## Availability and speed tiers

GPT-6.1 Sol is available through the OpenAI API and to eligible users in ChatGPT Work and Codex. OpenAI says it is not yet available in Chat. Standard and Fast processing are available, with Fast priced at twice Standard. OpenAI says Sol Ultrafast will arrive in the coming days, so it should not be described as live at launch.

## What the Critical rating means

Under OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework, GPT-6.1 Sol is treated as Critical capability in cybersecurity. That is a capability threshold, not a general claim that routine use is inherently unsafe or that security incidents are inevitable. OpenAI says the model uses the same safeguards stack as GPT-6 Astra.

## Practical implication

Teams should benchmark Standard and Fast against their own cost and latency needs and use appropriate access controls, logging and review when the model is allowed to take tool actions or modify systems.

## What to watch next

Watch for the actual launch and pricing of Sol Ultrafast, production evidence against OpenAI's benchmark claims, and any updates to the deployment-safety documentation.
