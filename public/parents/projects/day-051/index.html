Build your own with AI

Copy this beginner-friendly prompt into an AI assistant and ask it to explain each step:

Build me a mobile-friendly reading-fluency app in one standalone HTML file. Let a parent add and save custom passages, choose a reader name and text size, and run a timed reading session. Render every word as a tappable button so a grown-up can mark or unmark stumbles, optionally let the reader tap through words as a focus guide, then calculate elapsed time, words per minute, correct words per minute and accuracy. Save passages, settings and recent results only in local browser storage. Do not use a microphone, recording or external service.
Use with care. This app calculates from the words and stumbles a grown-up records. It is not a diagnostic reading assessment and should not be used to label or compare children.
