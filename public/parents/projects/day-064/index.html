Privacy and important limits

Supply names, quantities and notes are saved only in local storage in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded, no account is used and the app cannot order products, read shopping accounts or send reminders. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see the list, and clearing browser data may remove it.

The board only compares the numbers you enter. It cannot measure real stock or decide what your family needs. A grown-up must check quantities, product suitability, expiry dates, storage instructions and any safety-critical supplies.
