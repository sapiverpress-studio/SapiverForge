Friendly word play

The game uses a small built-in set of familiar words and checks every move instantly. A hint reveals one valid next step but adds to the hint count, so children can choose support without being stuck.

Words and scores are for informal family play rather than spelling or reading assessment.

Private by design

The player name and up to eight recent results stay in local browser storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, camera, microphone or external services.

Clearing browser data removes the saved results.
