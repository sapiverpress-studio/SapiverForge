### **Candidate [8]: Anthropic vs. OpenAI Revenue Accounting Discrepancy (Bloomberg)**
* **What is confirmed:** *Bloomberg* (Oct 9, 2026) reported that Anthropic and OpenAI calculate annualized revenue run-rates using different accounting conventions. Anthropic books sales through cloud partners (AWS, Google Cloud) on a **gross** basis (counting the full customer transaction value), while OpenAI records sales through partners (e.g., Microsoft) on a **net** basis (booking only its own cut). This difference created an apparent ~$18B–$20B divergence in headline revenue comparisons.
* **Important caveats:** Neither methodology represents accounting fraud; both gross and net reporting are valid standard practices depending on contract structure. However, unadjusted side-by-side comparison distorts their true relative scale.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **Yes.** Significant financial clarifyer for AI enterprise metrics and venture valuations.
