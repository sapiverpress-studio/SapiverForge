Private by design

Names, colours and measurements stay in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded and no account, advertising, camera, microphone, analytics or external service is used.

A grown-up enters and checks every measurement. The chart does not diagnose, predict adult height or compare a child with clinical growth standards; discuss health or growth concerns with an appropriate professional.
