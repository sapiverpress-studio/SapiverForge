### [8] DeepMind AlphaProof Code Formally Verifies Signal Protocol
* **Publisher:** deepmind.google / GitHub Pages
* **Editorial Scope:** IN SCOPE (AI Research & Cybersecurity)
* **Confirmed:** Google DeepMind released research introducing **AlphaProof Code**, a multi-agent AI system powered by Gemini Carbon. DeepMind used the system to generate roughly 6.5 million lines of machine-checked Lean proof code to formally verify key security properties (including post-quantum handshake and secrecy guarantees) of `libsignal` (Signal’s core cryptographic library) down to compiled AArch64 machine code.
* **Important Caveats:** Formal verification proves that machine code accurately satisfies the written formal specifications; it does not guarantee that specifications themselves are flaw-free. Human experts still had to review all formal specifications (approximately 1% of the codebase was flagged for human review).
* **Publication Verdict:** **Suitable for publication today.** High-impact milestone in combining LLM agentic workflows with formal software verification.

### [13] PLA-Linked Chinese University Obtained Nvidia H200 AI Chips

* **Editorial Scope:** IN SCOPE (AI Tech Regulation & Trade Control)
* **Confirmed:** Public Chinese procurement records reviewed by Reuters reveal that Northwestern Polytechnical University—a defense-focused university on the US Entity List—purchased two Nvidia H200 GPUs in October 2026 for 874,700 yuan (~$123,000) from domestic supplier Shaanxi Bosuan Network Technology. * **Publication Verdict:** **Suitable for publication today.** Major investigative reporting demonstrating enforcement gaps in US technology export bans.

### [5] VulnCheck Audit of Anthropic's Project Glasswing
* **Publisher:** vulncheck.com (Patrick Garrity)
* **Editorial Scope:** IN SCOPE (AI Security & Open-Source Infrastructure)
* **Confirmed:** Seven months after Anthropic launched its AI vulnerability scanner "Project Glasswing", VulnCheck tracked 442 CVEs attributed to Anthropic (~0.5% of all 2026 CVEs), with only 2 identified as exploited in the wild. Anthropic's public ledger lists 37,966 total findings, but only 7,349 are public and 217 marked fixed. Open-source maintainers (e.g., `curl`) report high false-positive rates (19 of 34 submitted reports rejected as non-vulnerabilities).
* **Important Caveats:** Independent audit based on public registries and Anthropic's ledger; does not invalidate AI bug discovery but highlights maintainer triage bottlenecks.
* **Publication Verdict:** **Suitable for publication today.** Provides valuable empirical counter-narrative data on automated AI vulnerability discovery.
