### Candidate [3]: Court denies motion to dismiss DRAM price fixing case
* **Scope**: IN SCOPE (Hardware market regulation, antitrust litigation, semiconductor supply chain).
* **What is confirmed**: In *Garciaguirre et al. v. Samsung Electronics Co., SK Hynix, & Micron Technology* (U.S. District Court, Northern District of California), the court denied the defendants' motion to dismiss. The class action alleges that the "Big Three" memory makers (controlling ~90% of global DRAM) coordinated legacy DDR3/DDR4 output cuts under the cover of pivoting capacity toward AI-focused High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), driving up consumer DRAM prices.
* **Important caveats**: Prior DRAM price-fixing class actions were dismissed by federal courts (e.g., in 2020/2022) due to failure to prove explicit collusion over parallel market behavior. Plaintiffs still face a high evidentiary burden at trial.
* **Recommendation**: **SUITABLE FOR PUBLICATION TODAY**. High impact on memory pricing and AI hardware infrastructure.
