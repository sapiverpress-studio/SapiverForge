Privacy, dates and important limits

Everything you enter is saved only in local storage in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded, and the app cannot read receipts, email, retailer accounts or device files. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see the saved entries. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Warranty dates are calculated from the purchase date and duration you enter. This tracker does not prove coverage or interpret terms, consumer rights, exclusions or repair eligibility. Always check the original receipt, retailer or manufacturer documents, and do not rely on this page as your only copy of important evidence.
