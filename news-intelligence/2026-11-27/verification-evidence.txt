#### **[6] Nvidia Blackwell Chips Discovered in Shanghai Data Center**
* **Publisher / Origin:** *Bloomberg* (Andy Lin, Mackenzie Hawkins, Kari Soo Lindberg)
* **What is confirmed:** Documents submitted to Chinese regulators in November 2026 reveal that Nvidia's export-restricted Blackwell hardware—including a GB300 NVL72 rack and GB200 servers—was installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project data center in Shanghai. Shanghai Shuoyao pledged the GB300 rack as collateral for a 96M yuan (~$14M) bank loan. Public serial numbers matched the assembly to Taiwanese server manufacturer Gigabyte. The site is linked to Megaspeed founder Alice Huang.
* **Important caveats:** Bloomberg noted no direct paper trail establishes how the hardware entered China, who originally purchased it from Gigabyte, or whether Megaspeed diverted it. Shipping Blackwell systems to Chinese entities requires an explicit US export license. Megaspeed denies wrongdoing.
* **Recommendation:** **SUITABLE FOR PUBLICATION TODAY.** Major investigative breaking news regarding AI hardware export control enforcement.
