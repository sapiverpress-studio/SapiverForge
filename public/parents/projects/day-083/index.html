Private by design

Your player name, choices and up to eight recent scores stay in local browser storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, camera, microphone or external services.

This is a playful drawing activity, not a handwriting, motor-skills or medical assessment. A grown-up should choose suitable challenges, encourage breaks and treat scores as informal game feedback.
