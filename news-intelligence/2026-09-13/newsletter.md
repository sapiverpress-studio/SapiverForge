## 2. California Enacts Strict Curbs on AI Chatbot Toys and Social Media

**Confirmed:** California Governor Gavin Newsom signed legislation banning AI companion chatbots in toys and prohibiting behaviourally addictive features on social media platforms for users under 16.

**Why it matters:** This legislation sets a significant regulatory precedent for how AI-integrated consumer products and digital platforms are designed for minors.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The package shows California treating AI-driven features in products used by children as a potential safety risk, and it may prompt legal challenges from technology trade groups.

**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/california-enacts-new-curbs-social-media-children-2026-09-11/) · confidence 90%

## 3. Partial Police Data Shows Rise in Recorded AI Deepfake Offences

**Confirmed:** Freedom of Information responses from 20 police forces in England and Wales recorded 163 offences containing terms including 'AI-generated', 'deepfake' and 'nudify' up to July 2026, compared with 10 across the same forces in 2023.

**Why it matters:** The partial figures show that participating police forces are recording substantially more incidents involving AI-related abuse than in 2023.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The figures do not establish whether the rise reflects more offending, improved identification and recording, or a combination of both.

**Source:** [Mark Tovey/Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2026/09/12/ai-nude-images-children-police-england-wales-deepfake/) · discovered via Techmeme · confidence 85%

## 4. Reuters Reports UAE Rethink of AI Data Centre Infrastructure

**Confirmed:** Reuters reports, citing six people familiar with the plans, that the UAE is considering shifting its 5-gigawatt AI data centre project from one campus to a distributed network that could include hardened underground facilities and dedicated air defences.

**Why it matters:** The reported proposals show how recent regional attacks have prompted officials to reconsider the physical resilience of critical AI infrastructure.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** If implemented, the redesign would show AI computing capacity being treated as strategic infrastructure requiring protection from physical threats.

**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/uae-revises-ai-data-center-plan-after-iranian-attacks-sources-say-2026-09-11/) · confidence 80%

## 5. Specific Labs Launches Real-SWE Benchmark for Enterprise Code

**Confirmed:** Specific Labs has released Real-SWE, a benchmark that evaluates AI coding agents on licensed private enterprise codebases with the aim of reducing training-set memorisation and benchmark contamination.

**Why it matters:** Using private codebases aims to test performance on less familiar software, but the benchmark's methodology and results still require independent scrutiny.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The release is one attempt to make coding-agent evaluation more representative of proprietary engineering work; it does not yet establish an industry-wide change.
