### **Candidate [1]: ARTEX AI agent converted to closed-source project following S. Korean bank breaches**
* **Publisher:** Reuters (Brenda Goh)
* **Status:** **IN SCOPE**
* **Confirmed:** Cybersecurity disclosures by CrowdStrike (Oct 7–8, 2026) confirmed that an open-source AI agentic penetration tool named **ARTEX** (developed by Chinese developer "Autumn-27") was utilized alongside LLMs (including DeepSeek, GLM, Grok, and Claude Code) in cyberattacks that exfiltrated data from several South Korean financial institutions (e.g., Shinhan Bank). Reuters reported on Oct 9, 2026, that the developer converted the ARTEX repository to a closed-source project following the reports.
* **Caveats:** CrowdStrike's attribution of the hacking campaign to a single 26-year-old Chinese speaker in Guangdong is made with moderate confidence. Closing the primary repository does not prevent threat actors from utilizing pre-existing forks or self-hosted instances.
* **Suitable for publication today:** **YES**

* **Publisher:** Bloomberg (Sarah Hilton)
* **Status:** **IN SCOPE**
