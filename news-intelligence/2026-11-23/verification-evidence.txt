* **What is Confirmed:**

#### **Candidate [13]: OpenAI Proposes Industry Pacing Limits Tied to "Perplexity" Metric**
* **Candidate Number:** [13]
* **What is Confirmed:** 
  * OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published a blog post on Nov 22, 2026, presenting historical held-out log-perplexity curves from GPT-3.5 through frontier models.
  * OpenAI proposed that frontier AI labs adopt log-perplexity as a standardized metric for measuring pretraining intelligence and establish voluntary industry pacing thresholds that labs agree not to cross until AI monitoring capabilities reach defined milestones.
* **Important Caveats:** 
  * This is an policy/safety proposal, not a binding cross-lab agreement or government regulation.
  * Perplexity evaluations reflect OpenAI's internal held-out datasets and cannot easily be compared across different lab pretraining corpora.
* **Suitable for Publication Today:** **YES**.
