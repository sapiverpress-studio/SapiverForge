* **What is confirmed:** On October 26, 2026, OpenAI published a blog post and preprints on GitHub claiming three AI-generated breakthroughs from an unreleased internal model across theoretical computer science (a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis), harmonic analysis (local smoothing for the wave equation), and quantum field theory.
