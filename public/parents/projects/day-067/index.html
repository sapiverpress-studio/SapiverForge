Privacy, creativity and grown-up controls

Comic titles, custom character names, panel contents and saved projects stay in local storage in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded and no account, camera, microphone or external service is used. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see saved comics.

A grown-up should check character names and writing for suitability before saving, printing or sharing. Downloaded PNG files leave the app and are then managed through the device’s normal files and sharing controls.
