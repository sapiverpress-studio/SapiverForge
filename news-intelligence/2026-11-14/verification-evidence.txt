#### **Candidate [4]: AI Helps Unlock Discoveries Hidden in Kurt Gödel’s Notebooks**
* **Scope:** IN SCOPE (AI for Science / Research Applications)
* **What is confirmed:** *The Guardian* reported that Finnish logician Jan von Plato (University of Helsinki) used Anthropic’s Claude to analyze 1,009 pages of Kurt Gödel’s 1940s shorthand notebooks (written in obsolete Gabelsberger script). Claude generated a catalogue of 663 potential theorems. When evaluated using the Coq formal proof verifier, ~400 were verified as stated, ~200 required minor formal adjustments, and ~50 were incorrect (aligning with Gödel's own marginal notes marking "falsch").
* **Important caveats:** Prominent logicians (such as Joel David Hamkins) emphasize that historical credit must be carefully parsed between Gödel’s original notes and Claude’s formal reconstructions. Gödel did not publish these results during his lifetime.
* **Publication assessment:** **SUITABLE FOR PUBLICATION TODAY.** Standout high-impact application of LLMs in historical mathematical research.
